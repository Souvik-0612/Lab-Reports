\begin{itemize}

\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{mA Linearity:} The Coefficient of Linearity (COL) was calculated as 0.010652, which is well within the acceptable limit of 0.1, indicating consistent radiation output across different mA stations.

\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}

\end{itemize}
