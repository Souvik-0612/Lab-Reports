The following specialized apparatus and instrumentation were employed during the execution of this experiment:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{HDR Brachytherapy Unit:} Specifically, a Microselectron remote afterloading system housing a high-dose-rate Iridium-192 ($^{192}$Ir) therapeutic source.
    \item \textbf{Dosimetry Phantom:} A custom-designed, solid slab Perspex (IPD) phantom, dimensioned $30 \times 30$ cm$^2$ with a 10 mm thickness.
    \item \textbf{Source Delivery Mechanism:} A standard transfer tube securely compatible with interstitial plastic catheters.
    \item \textbf{Radiation Detectors:} Thermoluminescent Dosimeter (TLD) capsules, specifically doped Calcium Sulfate (CaSO$_4$:Dy).
    \item \textbf{Readout Device:} A specialized TL/OSL Research Reader for processing the irradiated dosimeters.
\end{itemize}
