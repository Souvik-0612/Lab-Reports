The primary objectives of this experimental procedure are as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item To empirically determine and evaluate the fundamental TG-43 dosimetric parameters—specifically the Geometry Function $G(r,\theta)$, the Radial Dose Function $g(r)$, and the 2D Anisotropy Function $F(r,\theta)$—by executing in-phantom dosimetry on a clinical brachytherapy source.
    \item To investigate and delineate the underlying physical and geometric factors that influence these dosimetric parameters.
\end{enumerate}
