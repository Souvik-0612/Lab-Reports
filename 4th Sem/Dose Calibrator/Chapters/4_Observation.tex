\subsection{Precautions}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Wear gloves while handling radiopharmaceuticals and the dose calibrator.
	\item Carefully place source vial/syringe into the calibrator.
	\item Use L-bench while filling liquid radioisotope/radiopharmaceutical into syringe or vial.
	\item Wash hands with soap and water after the experiment.
	\item Always hold source vials using forceps/holding tongs.
	\item Switch on fume hood while handling evaporative pharmaceuticals.
\end{itemize}
