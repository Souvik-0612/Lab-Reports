\textbf{Pro-RF Basic tool:} The Pro-RF Basic tool is a quality assurance phantom designed for diagnostic X-ray machines. It is used to evaluate various parameters of X-ray imaging systems, including spatial resolution, contrast resolution, and image uniformity. The phantom typically consists of different test patterns and materials that simulate human tissue, allowing for comprehensive assessment of the imaging system's performance.
